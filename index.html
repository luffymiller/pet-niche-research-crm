Recommended keyword workflow

Use the same process for every micro-niche.
  1. Collect seed terms from the niche card and Google autocomplete / People Also Ask.
  2. Expand with Ahrefs, Semrush, Keyword Planner or another keyword database.
  3. Record US volume, KD, CPC and intent for each exact query.
  4. Manually inspect the top 10 results: page type, domain strength, topical match, forums and content gaps.
  5. Group close variants by search intent. One page should target one coherent intent cluster.
  6. Prioritize achievable long-tail queries, not just the highest volume.

Important guardrails

Cat and dog health/nutrition content can affect animal welfare. Avoid diagnosis, treatment instructions or unsupported diet claims. Cite authoritative veterinary sources and get qualified review for medical topics.
  • Do not label a niche “low competition” based on intuition.
  • Do not treat a tool's KD as a complete SERP analysis.
  • Separate product reviews from medical advice.
  • Use affiliate disclosures where required.
  • Disclose paid placements and qualify paid links with rel="sponsored" or nofollow.
