Starter hypotheses to test first

These are my judgement calls, not measured data. Verify each in the live Google US results before deciding.
  • Cat food is a crowded commercial space (Chewy, Petco, big brands, vet sites). Pick one narrow slice such as picky eaters, senior wet food, or protein-specific food, and build around comparisons and "how to switch" guides.
  • Dog behavior niches (separation anxiety, reactive dogs, pulling) are problem-led. Forums and small specialist sites often rank, so test these SERPs early.
  • Gear and home niches (catios, apartment cats, senior dog mobility) tend to monetize through affiliate comparisons and carry fewer health-claim risks than food.
  • Dog breeds are seeded as non-medical clusters (food products, training, grooming, gear, travel, behavior). Health topics are left out on purpose to avoid vet-credential issues.
  • Shortlist 3 to 5 niches, then compare their top-10 results before committing to one site.

SERP audit checklist

Do this for 5 to 10 keywords per niche.

Signs a SERP is beatable

  • Forums, Reddit or Quora threads in the top 10
  • Small or niche sites ranking with thin or outdated posts
  • Titles that only loosely match the query
  • Results older than 2 years on a fast-changing topic

Signs to skip or narrow

  • Top 10 filled with Chewy, Petco, AKC, vet clinics and major publishers
  • Every result is a deep, fresh, well-structured guide
  • Mostly product listing pages (you would need a store)

Guardrails

Pet health and nutrition content affects animal welfare. Avoid diagnosis and treatment advice, cite veterinary sources, disclose affiliate links, and mark paid links rel="sponsored".
